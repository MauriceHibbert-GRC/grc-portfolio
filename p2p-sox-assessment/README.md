# PwC Cybersecurity – P2P SOX Readiness Assessment (MedTech)

**Scenario:** As a junior associate in PwC’s Cybersecurity simulation, I reviewed a healthcare client’s Procure-to-Pay (P2P) SOP ahead of IPO to identify SOX-relevant control gaps and propose remediations.

## Objectives
- Review P2P SOP and related materials
- Identify missing/weak controls
- Map each gap to **SOX principle** and **ITGC** area
- Recommend practical, auditable fixes

## Deliverables
- **Findings Table** (Gap → Risk → SOX/ITGC → Recommendation)
- **Email Summary to Senior Associate**
- Notes on Internal Controls & ITGCs (learning artifacts)

## Quick Results (snapshot)
- Auto-approved requisitions → Missing authorization control
- No 3-way match → Completeness & accuracy risk
- Shared GL credentials → No accountability/audit trail
- Single-person check signing → Segregation of duties failure
- Privileged access via verbal approval → No evidence of authorization
- Access requests via email → Weak provisioning & least-privilege
