# PwC Cybersecurity – P2P SOX Readiness Assessment (MedTech)

**Scenario:** Reviewed a healthcare client’s Procure-to-Pay (P2P) SOP ahead of IPO to identify SOX-relevant control gaps and propose remediations.

## Objectives
- Review the P2P SOP and related materials
- Identify missing or weak controls
- Map each gap to SOX principles and ITGC areas
- Provide PwC-style recommendations

## Deliverables
- Findings Table (Gap → Risk → SOX/ITGC → Recommendation)
- Email Summary to Senior Associate
- Reflections on key learnings
